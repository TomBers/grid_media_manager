## Production and review

For each story: generate a private RationalGrid source; inspect the completed nodes and synthesis; create a campaign; produce a concise paced vertical video for Instagram/TikTok/YouTube, substantive LinkedIn/Facebook copy with cover and CTA images, and a focused X idea card and CTA. Choose photographic covers from the existing Pexels workflow. Review grounding, qualifications, spelling, pacing and platform limits before rendering and scheduling. Preserve the link between each story and its evidence.

Private-grid destination links need special attention before public posting: a private source is not automatically accessible to a social audience. Confirm the intended public destination or publish the source through an authorized RationalGrid workflow before scheduling. The MCP server does not expose publication.
